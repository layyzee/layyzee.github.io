## 📦 What Each Project Proves to Recruiters & Hiring Managers

### 1. [BalaBoard](https://github.com/layyzee/balaboard)
* **Agile Competency:** Eliminates meeting friction in Sprint Retrospectives; saves $16/seat SaaS whiteboard fees.
* **Key Features:** Low-latency WebSockets, 12 Miro retrospective frameworks, offline AI semantic clustering, 1-click Jira Cloud issue creation with persistent canvas badges.
* **Facilitator Controls:** 5-minute timer with alarm, hidden sticky notes to prevent bias, 1-click Recruiter Sandbox.

### 2. [SprintPoker](https://github.com/layyzee/SprintPoker)
* **Agile Competency:** Eliminates cognitive anchoring bias during Backlog Refinement and Sprint Planning.
* **Key Features:** Oval felt poker table with trigonometric player seating, 3D face-down cards, automated consensus detection with confetti, Web Audio synthesizer, RFC 4180 Jira CSV backlog export.
* **Decks Supported:** Fibonacci, Modified Fibonacci, T-Shirt Sizing, Powers of 2.

### 3. [PI Planner](https://github.com/layyzee/PIPlanner)
* **Agile Competency:** Definitive proof of **SAFe® 6 SSM** certification and multi-team scaling experience (Incedo/Verizon ART).
* **Key Features:** Multi-sprint program swimlanes across 4 squads, dynamic curved red dependency strings, dedicated gold Innovation & Planning (IP) sprint, 4-column ROAM Risk matrix (Resolved, Owned, Accepted, Mitigated), all-hands Fist of Five confidence vote.
* **Secret Weapon:** Built-in SAFe 6.0 Interview Cheat Sheet with word-for-word interview scripts.

### 4. [Dia Studio](https://github.com/layyzee/DiaStudio)
* **Agile Competency:** Advanced AI Tooling & Autonomous Knowledge Work Orchestration.
* **Key Features:** Clean-room reconstruction of The Browser Company's Dia Browser (`company.thebrowser.dia`); incorporates Bun agent runtime with embedded Claude Code harness, 43 specialized subagents, numbered DOM browser-use automation with human-in-the-loop 2FA handoffs, and generative SlideKit presentation carousels.

### 5. [BalaGPT](https://github.com/layyzee/chatgpt-web)
* **Agile Competency:** Technical literacy and personal AI productivity enablement.
* **Key Features:** Groq Llama-3.3 70B streaming inference, zero-external-DB Node 22 native SQLite storage, multi-page PDF document extraction, DuckDuckGo web search.
