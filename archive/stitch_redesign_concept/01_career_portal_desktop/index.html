50% Presumptive Expense

Under Section 44ADA, professionals earning up to ₹75 Lakhs gross receipts can declare a flat 50% as business operational expenses (zero audit or receipts required). Tax applies strictly to remaining 50%.

0% GST via LUT Export

Exporting software consultancy and agile delivery management services to overseas clients qualifies as "Export of Services". By filing an online Letter of Undertaking (LUT), you pay 0% GST completely legally.
